Samsung Art Mode CLI (`samsung-artmode`) is free, open-source software maintained by SpaceCorps.

- **Source Code**: [GitHub](https://github.com/SpaceCorps/Samsung-Artmode-Cli)

## Hardware & TV Connectivity
The CLI interacts directly with your Samsung The Frame TV over your local area network (LAN/Wi-Fi):
- **Zero Cloud Subscriptions**: Operates entirely over local network WebSocket (ports 8001/8002) and TCP sockets without intermediary third-party cloud services.
- **Personal Artwork Uploads**: Completely free to upload and display your own JPEG/PNG photographs.
- **Samsung Art Store**: Viewing custom uploaded art does not require a Samsung Art Store subscription.
